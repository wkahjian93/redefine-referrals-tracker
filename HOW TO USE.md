# REDEFINE Referrals Tracker — how to use

**The problem it fixes:** agents don't report their referrals because typing them up is *mafan*, so you can't count them or pay the bonus fairly.
**How:** the agent takes one photo of the referral book page. The app reads and counts the names, hides client privacy, posts it to the team board, cheers the agent on, and keeps the count for bonus. The agent types nothing.

---

## For agents (30 seconds per page)

1. **Open the app** → tap the gold **📷 Snap** button.
2. **Take a photo** of the filled referral page (flat, good light, whole page in the photo). Or pick one from your gallery.
3. Wait about 15–20 seconds. The app:
   - finds every referred name and **counts them for you**
   - puts **black boxes** over phone numbers, ages, remarks (L/W/R/S) and your client's own name
   - puts a **🙂** over any face
4. **Check the photo.** Drag your finger over anything still showing to cover it. Tap a box to uncover it (only if it covers a name by mistake).
   Names that were hard to read are shown in **yellow**. Fix the spelling if needed.
5. Tick **"I checked the photo…"** → **Post** → **Post it**.
6. 🎉 You get your encouragement, and the team sees your update straight away. Tap **Share to WhatsApp** if you want to tell the group. WhatsApp opens and you pick the group.

**Got names at a roadshow?** Tap 📷 Snap → **🎪 Roadshow name list**:
1. Type the **📍 roadshow location** (the app remembers places you used before).
2. Take a **🤳 selfie at the booth** as proof. Your face stays visible; other people's faces are covered 🙂 automatically.
3. Snap the **name list**. Everything after that is the same as a referral page: names counted, private details covered, leader approves, RM1 per name once you call within 14 days, ☕ appointment RM5, 🎯 close RM20.

**Asked but got no names?** Snap → **🙋 I asked a client but got no names**. It counts as an *Approach* (effort, not paid).

**After the referral (this is where the bigger bonus is):** the **📋 Leads** tab (bottom bar).
- Met the person? Tap **☕ Appointment done** (RM5 after leader approval).
- Closed a case? Tap **🎯 Closed case** (RM20 after leader approval). You need the appointment marked first.
- Tapped by mistake? Tap **undo** (only while it's still waiting approval).

**Your monthly bonus:** Board → **Your referral bonus** shows your row of the bonus sheet. It updates live, and after your leader pays you'll see "paid ✅".

**Reminders** appear at the top of your Feed (and pop up once a day):
- ⏰ last 3 days of the month: update everything before the bonus month closes
- 📒 no referral page yet this month (from the 5th)
- ☕ leads waiting more than a week for an appointment update
- 💰 early in the month: last month's bonus amount and whether it's paid

**Other tabs**
- **📋 Leads:** your names to call (red badge = still to call), appointments and closes.
- **🏠 Feed:** everyone's referral updates. Comment to cheer each other on.
- **📊 Board:** leaderboard, weekly trend and team numbers (the data analysis). "Board talk" is for comments on the team's effort.
- **👤 Me:** your totals, background music ♪, sounds, phone alerts.

**What your teammates can and can't see**

| Everyone sees | Only you + leader see | Never kept |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, the referred names, the count, the covered photo, comments | Age, phone, gender, remarks, the referring client's name | The original uncovered photo |

**30 days after the leader approves a page**, its photos (the covered name list and the roadshow selfie) are deleted automatically to save space. The names and your call / appointment / close records stay.

**After 3 months**, the phone number, age, gender, remarks, the referring client's name and the photo are deleted automatically. Only the referred names stay.

## For the leader

- **Board → "N pages waiting for your approval" → Review.** Tick the names that count for bonus, then **Save decision**. Possible duplicates (same phone number or same name already on the board) start **unticked** and show ⚠️.
- **Board → "N appointment / close updates waiting" → Review.** Untick anything that didn't really happen, then **Save decisions**. Approved items post a celebration card to the team.
- **Board → Team referral bonus:** your monthly bonus sheet, in the same layout as before: Name · Approach · Leads Obtain (RM1) · Successful Appointment (RM5) · Close Case (RM20) · Total. Pick any of the last 12 months.
  - **Download spreadsheet (CSV)** for your records.
  - After you pay: **Mark [month] as paid**. Each agent sees their paid amount. If late approvals change a paid month, the app warns you.
  - **Remind team on WhatsApp** prepares a message with the rates, the days left and who hasn't posted this month.
- **Your reminders** (top of Feed, pop-up once a day): items waiting approval; last month's bonus ready to pay (1st–10th) until you mark it paid; month closing in 5 days → send the WhatsApp reminder.
- **Me → Leader settings:** the three rates (default RM1 / RM5 / RM20 from your sheet). Approach is counted, not paid.
- **Which month an item counts in:** a lead counts in the month the page was posted, an appointment or close in the month the agent tapped it. Only approved items are paid.
- **Me → Team members:** remove someone who left. Their past referrals stay on the board.
- An agent can delete their own page only while it is waiting or not counted. Once approved, only you can delete it.
- Everything important (posting, approving, deleting, changing the rate, removing a member) asks **"Are you sure?"** first.

## Install on the phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open in Chrome → ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.
- First time: team code (from leader) + your name + choose a 4-digit PIN.

## Good to know
- **Alerts:** pop-up + sound + red badge while the app is open. In **Me → Phone pop-up alerts → Turn on**, it also alerts while the app is in the background (Android; iPhone only after Add to Home Screen). Alerts when the app is fully closed are not built yet.
- **WhatsApp:** WhatsApp doesn't allow an app to post into a group by itself. The button prepares the message and you choose the group. Shared messages never include client names or numbers.
- **Music:** the team song "转介绍" plays when the app opens (phones start it on your first tap). Turn it off with ♪ at the top or in Me; it stays off until you turn it back on.
- If the AI can't read a photo, you can **Try again** or **Type the names myself** (then you must cover the private details by dragging).
